# Glossary

**Agent**  
A persistent individual NPC with private state.

**Belief**  
An agent's uncertain representation of a proposition, separate from objective truth.

**Chunk**  
A fixed-size block of detailed world cells generated and loaded together.

**Community language**  
Statistics derived from overlapping personal language usage.

**Concept**  
An internal semantic category or representation.

**Construction**  
A learned probabilistic pattern connecting a signal sequence to semantic roles.

**Cold state**  
Personal information rarely accessed, such as old memories and uncommon vocabulary.

**Consolidation**  
Conversion of detailed memories into summaries, beliefs, skills, or relationship values.

**Desired effect**  
What a speaker privately wants communication to achieve.

**Discrete-event simulation**  
A simulation that processes meaningful events at scheduled times rather than updating all state every tick.

**Event-driven cognition**  
An agent thinks when triggered by a threshold, observation, interaction, or obligation.

**Feature**  
A sparse world object such as a tree, boulder, bush, building, or ruin.

**Grounding**  
Learning meaning through perception, action, needs, context, and consequences.

**Hot state**  
Frequently accessed agent data such as next event, position, activity, and immediate needs.

**Interpretation hypothesis**  
A possible meaning assigned to an observed signal, with a score or probability.

**Lexicon**  
An individual's associations between signals and concepts.

**Personal language**  
The exact language knowledge of one agent.

**Private intent**  
The sender's internal meaning or desired outcome, unavailable to receivers.

**Public signal**  
Observable vocal, gestural, visual, or physical communication data.

**Region**  
A larger world partition used for ownership, scheduling, and parallel processing.

**Repair**  
Clarification behavior after uncertain or failed communication.

**Signal**  
An observable sound, gesture, marking, movement, or other form.

**Sparse pool**  
A packed external store for variable-length records owned by agents.

**Statistical aggregation**  
Replacing individuals with group counts or distributions. This design avoids doing so for persistent NPC identity.

**Timing wheel**  
A bucketed event scheduler suitable for large numbers of future events.

**World delta**  
A saved modification applied on top of deterministic procedural generation.
